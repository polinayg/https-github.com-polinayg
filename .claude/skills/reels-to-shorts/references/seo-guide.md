# YouTube Shorts SEO guide

Instagram captions are written for followers scrolling a feed. YouTube is a search engine:
the title and description need the words people actually type. Rewrite – don't copy-paste.

## Before writing: pick the keyword

1. Work out what the reel is *about* in search terms (e.g. "instagram reel ideas for small
   business", not "my Tuesday vibes ✨").
2. Choose **one primary keyword** (2–5 words) and 2–4 related secondary keywords.
3. Prefer specific, intent-driven phrases: how-to, tips, ideas, before/after, tutorial,
   "for beginners", tool or brand names, places.

## Title (≤ 100 chars; aim for 40–70)

- Primary keyword in the **first ~40 characters** (that's what shows on mobile).
- A hook: number, outcome, curiosity or contrast. "3 Reel Hooks That Doubled My Views".
- End with ` #Shorts`.
- No clickbait the video doesn't deliver, no ALL CAPS, at most one emoji, no `<` `>`.

## Description (≤ 5000 chars; aim for 150–400 words)

Structure:

1. **Lines 1–2 (shown above "more")**: restate the hook with the primary keyword in the
   first sentence. This is the most important text after the title.
2. **2–4 short sentences** of what the viewer learns/sees, naturally using secondary
   keywords. Write for humans; no keyword stuffing or repeated lists.
3. **Call to action**: subscribe / comment a question / watch a related video.
4. **Links** the user wants (website, other socials). Ask once if unknown; never invent.
5. **Hashtags**: 3–5 relevant ones on the last line, `#Shorts` first. YouTube shows the
   first three above the title; more than 15 makes YouTube ignore all of them.

Strip Instagram-only things: "link in bio", @mentions that don't exist on YouTube,
walls of 30 hashtags, "double tap".

## Tags (total ≤ 500 chars)

8–15 tags: primary keyword, secondary keywords, common misspellings or variations, the
creator/brand name, and a couple of broad category tags. Tags matter little for ranking,
so don't overthink them.

## Quality check before saving each file

- Would someone searching the primary keyword click this title?
- Does the description's first sentence contain the primary keyword?
- Is every claim true to what's in the video?
- Hashtags ≤ 5, `#Shorts` included, nothing Instagram-specific left over.
